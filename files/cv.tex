\hfill Mar 2026--Present

\textit{Advisor: Prof. Hideo Kaiju, Keio University}
\hfill Kanagawa, Japan

\begin{itemize}

signs for the two enantiomers at room temperature.
\item Built a LabVIEW-automated, cryostat-integrated four-probe MR measurement system and extended MR measurements down to 50 K to investigate \highlight{the temperature dependence of BINOL-based CISS}.

\hfill Feb--Jul 2026

\textit{Advisor: Prof. Junichiro Kono, Rice University}
\hfill Houston, TX (On-site: Feb--Mar 2026)
\begin{itemize}
\item \highlight{Competitively selected for a fully funded research internship with a stipend.}
\item Collaborated on the assembly of gated devices using

\item Performed gate-dependent optical transmission spectroscopy,

intersubband plasmon peaks in single-chirality carbon nanotube films.

enabling the identification of transition peaks resolved in single-chirality
carbon nanotubes and demonstrating quantitative
agreement with experimental spectra.
